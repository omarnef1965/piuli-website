piuli

Pew-lee · means “beautiful”

A beautiful life is made of beautiful moments.

Piuli is a free iPhone app that gathers the stories, talks and moments that lift people up, twelve at a time. Each story sits behind one word. Watch it, earn the word, and find the greatness already in you.

Every video plays from the official YouTube channel of its owner, who keeps all rights and credit.
