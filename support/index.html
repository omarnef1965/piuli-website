Common questions

How does a word become mine?
Watch the story to the end. Skipping ahead does not count; the word is yours after watching most of the selected part.

Where are my reflections and recordings?
Only on your iPhone, in My journey. You can delete a recording there at any time.

How do I change or stop the daily reminder?
In Piuli, open Settings (the sliders icon) and use “Remind me each day”.

How do I start over?
Settings → Clear my progress.

Can I suggest a video or a collection?
Yes: tap “Suggest a video” in the app, or the More tile on the home screen.
